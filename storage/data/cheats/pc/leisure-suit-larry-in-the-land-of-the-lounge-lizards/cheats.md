## 1. Age & Copy Protection Bypass

### The Trivia Skip Code
When the game starts and the original 5 age-verification trivia questions begin:
* Press **Ctrl + Alt + X** simultaneously on your keyboard.
* This will immediately skip the pop-culture quiz.
* **Note:** The game will still force you to answer exactly ONE final question taken directly from the original physical manual inserts ("feelies"). Use the master list below to answer it.

## 3. Money Exploits & Gameplay Cheats

### The Casino "Save & Reload" Infinite Money Trick
Because the 1991 VGA edition lacks a developer debug mode console for direct wallet manipulation, the standard way to max out your money is via the slot machines or blackjack table inside the casino:
1. Walk into the Casino and stand in front of a slot machine or the blackjack table.
2. Save your game immediately (e.g., name the save slot "Money").
3. Bet the maximum amount ($20).
4. **If you win:** Immediately save your game again over your previous slot.
5. **If you lose:** Open the menu and restore/reload your "Money" save slot. 
6. Repeat this process until you have enough cash to buy all inventory items ($200 to $300 is more than enough to beat the entire game comfortably).

### Critical Survival Rules (Anti-Softlock)
* **Pay the Cabbie:** Every single time you use the taxi, you **must** use your wallet on the cab driver *before* opening the door to exit. If you step out without paying, Larry will instantly be run over and killed.
* **Use the Breath Spray:** Always use the breath spray on Larry before talking to any of the female characters. If your breath smells bad, they will reject you outright, preventing puzzle progression.
* **Buy the Condom (Lubricant):** Do not forget to purchase the "lubricant" from the convenience store clerk before attempting to interact with Eve in the hotel penthouse.
