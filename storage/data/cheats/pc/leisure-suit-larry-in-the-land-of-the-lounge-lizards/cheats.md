## Skip age check & intros
- 1991 VGA remake: To skip the age-verification trivia quiz, press Ctrl + Alt + X during the opening questionnaire.

## Phone-number skip
- Enter phone number code 555-0724 or 0724 on startup prompts in related early Sierra titles to bypass sequences.

## Gameplay tips
- Easy money: Save before playing blackjack or using slot machines; reload if you lose.
- Taxi: Always pay the taxi driver when arriving at a destination before exiting the cab, or Larry will die.
- Fresh breath: Use your breath spray if the game reminds you about Larry's bad breath.
